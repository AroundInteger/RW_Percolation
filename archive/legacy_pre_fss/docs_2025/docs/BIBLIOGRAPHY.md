## Citation Format

All references should be formatted according to the target journal's style guide. For most physics and materials science journals, the format is:

**Journal Articles**:
Author, A. B. (Year). "Title of article." *Journal Name*, Volume(Issue), Page range.

**Books**:
Author, A. B. (Year). *Title of Book*. Edition. Publisher.

**Conference Proceedings**:
Author, A. B. (Year). "Title of paper." In *Proceedings of Conference Name* (pp. Page range). Publisher.
